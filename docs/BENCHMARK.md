# Benchmark results

Generated by `python -m architectural_converter.benchmark.run_benchmark` (also shown live in the web app's *Benchmark* tab).

**Important:** these drawings are *synthetic*: they are generated from plan specifications by `benchmark/dxf_writer.py`, which is what makes
the reference geometry exact. They deliberately vary units, layer conventions, wall representations, blocks, hatches,
fragmentation and duplicates, but they are cleaner than most real-world drawings. They prove the algorithms work and
protect against regressions; they do **not** measure accuracy on your real projects. To do that, add real DXF files with
manually verified reference geometry to `architectural_converter/benchmark/drawings/` (same JSON format).

Metric definitions: wall precision/recall are length-based (centre lines sampled every 5 cm, correct within max(0.6·t, 8 cm)
of a reference axis with a compatible direction). Rooms are matched at IoU ≥ 0.5 (boundary accuracy = mean IoU). Doors and
windows are matched by centre distance ≤ max(w/2, 0.3 m). NEEDS_REVIEW elements count as detected.
